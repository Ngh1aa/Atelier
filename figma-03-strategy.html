03 / UX & product strategy

Expression expands.
Friction contracts.

Hypothesis-led product direction

The core product thesis is progressive clarity: stronger editorial expression at discovery, then increasingly disciplined product and transaction surfaces as risk and commitment rise.

Problem statement

How might ATELIER create desire without making size, fit, variant, availability and purchase decisions feel hidden or theatrical?

Primary JTBD

When I am considering a piece, help me understand how it looks, fits and can be purchased so I can decide without breaking the fashion experience.

01

Desire at discovery

Use photography, composition and editorial pacing where browsing is the job.

02

Evidence at decision

Price, variant, size, fit, availability, delivery and returns move closer to commitment.

03

State honesty

Do not turn unknown inventory, simulated payment or local-only records into production claims.

04

Motion with a job

Hierarchy, continuity, feedback or state change only; no scroll-jacking or motion-only information.
