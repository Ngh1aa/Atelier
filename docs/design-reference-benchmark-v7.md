# ATELIER V7 — Design Reference Benchmark

## Project decision
The previous white-editorial pass changed palette and typography more than composition. V7 must create visible, page-role-specific redesign delta while preserving the static/local commerce reality and existing product data.

## Reference synthesis

| Reference | Role | Principle extracted | Do not copy | Atelier adaptation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TOTEME | Homepage / campaign commerce | Campaign tiles and product/collection entry points can carry the homepage without a conventional centered hero | Brand-specific typography, exact tile ordering, product photography | Use an asymmetric contact-sheet composition with existing Atelier imagery, direct shoppable labels and crop variation |
| Studio Nicholson | Home + PLP | Product appears early; quick-add/product utility remains visible inside a strong editorial brand | Exact product-card styling or store/journal content | Make New In part of the first two viewport lengths; use dense catalogue rhythm and persistent product utility |
| ARKET | Editorial + seasonal curation | White space works when it structures content; editorial and catalogue are separate content modes | ARKET's own identity, typeface and editorial taxonomy | Use white space as measured gutters/rules, not as empty padding; alternate campaign and decision modules |
| COS | PLP | Listing pages prioritize product finding, count, filters and product scanability | Exact merchandising grid | Keep Shop header compact and utility-led; product grid gets stronger image scale and less copy |
| The Row | Navigation / restraint | Minimal chrome makes product and imagery primary | Brand-specific ultra-minimal opacity and content gaps | Navigation remains quiet, but page composition does the differentiation |

## Page-role reference matrix

| Page role | User question | Reference role | Principle | Atelier-specific composition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Home | What is this collection and where do I enter? | TOTEME + Studio Nicholson | Campaign first, shoppable immediately | Full-bleed campaign image + oversized collection label + offset secondary crop + 3 product strip |
| Shop | What can I buy and how do I narrow it? | COS + Studio Nicholson | Catalogue first | Compact masthead, sticky utility rail, denser product grid, no hero block |
| PDP | Is this piece right for me? | Premium DTC / editorial commerce | Image object + decision rail | Large product canvas with sticky purchase/fitting rail; service evidence immediately below action |
| Checkout | What do I need to provide and what happens? | Utility commerce pattern | Remove editorial distraction | Compact progress header + form sheet + sticky summary, no campaign treatment |

## Extracted design DNA
- Layout grammar: 12-column editorial grid, deliberate edge-to-edge sections, large image planes, thin rules, numbered captions.
- Page-role diversity: Home = campaign contact sheet; Shop = catalogue; PDP = fitting desk; Checkout = utility sheet.
- Typography: display serif only for campaign statements; UI/product names use neutral sans; avoid serif on every heading.
- Color: white/ink remains neutral system; imagery supplies chroma. No invented brand accent.
- Media: aggressive crop variation, 4:5 portrait, 16:10 landscape, occasional full-bleed crop.
- Motion: reveal by clip/translate, image parallax only where lightweight; no decorative motion on checkout.
- Mobile: recompose, not stack desktop. Home uses full-width campaign frames; Shop stays two-column product grid; PDP image first then decision sheet; Checkout single-column.
