# ATELIER V15 — PLANNED VALIDATION

**Status: PLANNED VALIDATION — no participant findings exist yet.**

This protocol is intentionally separate from the expert audit and production-reference benchmark. Nothing below is evidence that users have completed these tasks successfully.

## Objective

Test whether the V15 editorial-to-commerce journey preserves desire while making product and purchase decisions easy to understand.

## Tasks

1. Find a specific item from the current collection.
2. Choose the intended colour/variant and an available size.
3. Determine fit, delivery and return information before purchase.
4. Add the product to Bag and continue through the checkout prototype.

## What to observe

- route chosen from editorial entry to product;
- whether Search / Filter / Sort are discoverable when needed;
- hesitation or errors around colour and size;
- whether unavailable/unknown stock language is understood;
- whether Size Guide, fit and model information are found without prompting;
- whether delivery/return reassurance is found before Add to Bag;
- whether mini-Bag, Bag and Checkout feel like one continuous task;
- keyboard/focus issues for participants who navigate without a pointer;
- whether motion helps orientation or feels like delay.

## Moderator rules

- Do not explain the interface before each task.
- Ask participants to think aloud without steering them to a control.
- If they are blocked, record the point before offering the minimum recovery prompt.
- Distinguish content uncertainty from interaction uncertainty.
- Do not describe simulated checkout/payment as real processing.

## Evidence to capture

For each task, record completion path, wrong turns, recovery, questions asked, notable hesitation, and direct participant comments. Time-on-task may be recorded descriptively; no benchmark threshold is assumed in advance.

## Accessibility inclusion

Include keyboard-only passes for the critical journey. When participant access is available, include assistive-technology/reflow checks appropriate to the audience instead of treating automated Axe output as participant validation.

## Exit

After sessions, findings must be labeled by participant/task and separated from expert observations. Only observed evidence may update the hypothesis or be reported as usability findings.
