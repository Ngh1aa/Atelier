# ATELIER V14 — Design Reference Benchmark

## Phase classification

- Scope: `whole-site`
- Type: `redesign`
- Risk: `medium`
- Mode: `interactive_prototype`
- Responsive scope: `desktop_only` — inherited from current project truth; mobile/tablet remain `N/A_JUSTIFIED` unless explicitly reopened.

## Decision problem

Evolve the passed V13 attainable-youth-luxury system into a more desirable high-fashion identity for younger customers while preserving the existing static ecommerce journey, URLs, truthful system reality and useful V13 interaction behavior.

## V14 synthesis

### ADOPT

- Large media fields with low interface chrome.
- Clear distinction between campaign/story pages and product-decision pages.
- Compact product metadata and direct collection/category entry.
- Editorial world-building linked to actual products.
- Strong whitespace and disciplined navigation.

### ADAPT

- V13 cobalt signal becomes **deep oxblood** to feel less tech/utility-led while preserving one recognizable action/state accent.
- V13 ivory becomes a slightly cooler **porcelain** field; near-black becomes the dominant campaign contrast field.
- Home retains the V13 poster concept but changes from a fixed 7/5 split to an immersive campaign canvas with an overlapping fashion-house panel.
- Shop retains filtering/search/quick-add but shifts from four compact columns to a three-column editorial catalogue at the evidence width.
- PDP retains complete-garment media and truthful purchase data, but makes the media stage visually dominant and the purchase rail more restrained.

### REJECT

- Glassmorphism, gradients, rounded SaaS cards, routine shadows.
- All-black site treatment that reduces product distinction.
- Decorative serif everywhere.
- Fake scarcity, fake exclusivity or unverified luxury service promises.
- One universal fashion hero reused across Shop, PDP, Services and Checkout.

## Visual taste calibration

- Memorable commitment: **porcelain / ink / oxblood + runway indexing + asymmetrical campaign framing**.
- Generic tells found in V13: equal three-card rhythm, familiar split hero, utility cobalt reading as digital-product signal, four-column PLP density that can feel more mass-catalogue than fashion edit.
- KEEP: hard rules, full-garment imagery, compact labels, authored index language, zero-radius discipline.
- REVISE: campaign composition, palette roles, PLP scale, product-decision hierarchy.
- REMOVE: cobalt as universal action accent on representative V14 routes; excessive equal-card rhythm.
