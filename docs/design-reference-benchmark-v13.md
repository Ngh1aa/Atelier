# ATELIER V13 — Design Reference Benchmark

## Decision problem

Create an attainable-luxury ecommerce experience for young Vietnamese earners without turning ATELIER into a promotion-heavy fast-fashion site or cloning a global luxury shell.

## Evidence and sources inspected

| Reference | Type | Page/state inspected | Job | Principle kept | Do not copy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [COS](https://www.cos.com/en-ww) | production brand | Home + Women/New Arrivals search-rendered content | brand/editorial restraint | campaign media can coexist with direct Women/Men/shop entry and concise collection language | country gate, global-brand scale, sparse product context |
| [Massimo Dutti Vietnam](https://www.massimodutti.com/vn/) | production competitor | Home/category search-rendered content | premium catalogue orientation | category vocabulary and a clear new-collection path within a polished visual system | promotion-led seasonal content and identical brand surface |
| [Mango Vietnam clothing](https://shop.mango.com/vn/en/c/women/clothing/e38a534b) | production price/category peer | PLP with filters, grid-density controls, prices and color options | target price reality + catalogue utility | 899K–3.999M VND observed price band supports an attainable-premium benchmark for the stated salary segment; visible filter/order controls | SEO copy volume, sale language and merchandising density that would dilute ATELIER |
| [Aritzia](https://www.aritzia.com/intl/en) | production adjacent brand | Home/category/brand navigation content | wardrobe-by-context | premium can feel young through wardrobe occasions and multiple in-house aesthetics, not only serif/white-space conventions | scale, copy tone and unverified service promises |
| [Baymard Product List UX 2025](https://baymard.com/blog/current-state-product-list-and-filtering) | research benchmark | product-list findings | PLP usability | filtering, sorting and product-list usability are core product discovery infrastructure; mobile requires specific attention | universal thresholds or claims that ATELIER itself was user-tested |
| [Baymard Product Page UX](https://baymard.com/blog/current-state-ecommerce-product-page-ux) | research benchmark | PDP findings | decision confidence | product media and decision information must accumulate confidence before Add to Bag | copying a checklist without fitting the local catalogue |

## Reference-to-page-role matrix

| ATELIER role | User question | Reference principle | V13 adaptation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | “Is this my style and can I enter quickly?” | COS restraint + Aritzia wardrobe contexts | editorial poster opening plus three shoppable life moments and explicit price/value bridge |
| Shop | “How do I narrow this edit?” | Mango/Massimo catalogue utility + Baymard PLP evidence | compact intro, visible controls, 4-column desktop/2-column mobile grid, persistent applied filters |
| PDP | “Is this worth my budget and right for me?” | Baymard decision confidence | complete garment frame, price + pay-cycle relevance without financing claims, fit/material/service before commitment |
| Collections | “How do I wear this point of view?” | COS/Massimo campaign-to-commerce bridge | look chapters linked to Workday/After Hours/Weekend products |
| The House | “Why trust this point of view?” | COS/Aritzia concise brand systems | house codes and wardrobe philosophy, no invented history or sustainability claims |
| Checkout | “What happens next?” | conventional ecommerce transaction clarity | distraction-reduced local prototype checkout with transparent status |

## Final synthesis

- **Layout grammar:** strong editorial first anchor on Home/Collections; utility-led Shop; media/decision split PDP; compact transaction family.
- **Type:** modern grotesk/sans carries most hierarchy; serif appears only as a human editorial interruption.
- **Color:** ink/ivory base, one electric cobalt signal for current state and action—not large decorative fields.
- **Media:** portrait/full-body, white/quiet backgrounds, consistent perceived garment scale within each family.
- **Price position:** attainable premium reference band, not luxury-price theatre.
- **Motion:** immediate visibility first; motion only adds orientation after the content is already readable.
