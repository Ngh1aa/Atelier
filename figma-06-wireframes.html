06 / Wireframes & exploration

Compose the tension.
Then choose the rule.

PDP structure · editorial vs transaction

ATELIER’s key exploration question was not visual decoration. It was where editorial expression should stop yielding space to size, fit, variant, availability and purchase evidence.

PDP exploration

Three structural directions.

The selected system preserves media scale while giving decision controls a stable, readable rail.

A / Campaign-firstRejected

Strong desire, but size/fit and purchase controls arrive too late. Beautiful, yet costly when the user is ready to decide.

B / Catalogue-firstRejected

Very explicit, but the fashion-house expression collapses into a generic ecommerce product template.

C / Editorial + railSelected

Large garment media supports desire; a narrow decision rail keeps identity, price, colour, size, fit, state and action continuously legible.

Home

Asymmetry allowed

Campaign imagery, indexed microtype and negative space create a point of view.

Shop

Scan rhythm first

Product identity and filters stay predictable; hover adds affordance but not essential information.

PDP

Media + decision rail

Emotion and evidence coexist instead of competing for the same visual role.

Checkout

Ledger, not campaign

Progress, validation and order summary take precedence over decorative storytelling.

Selected visual signature: severe rules, indexed microtype, hard edges, complete-garment media, restrained monochrome surfaces and a deliberate shift from editorial asymmetry to transactional order as commitment rises.
