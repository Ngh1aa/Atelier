01 / Project overview

Desire first.
Clarity at commitment.

Editorial luxury × transactional discipline

ATELIER is a responsive luxury-fashion commerce prototype exploring a deliberate tension: discovery can be expressive, but product and transaction decisions must become progressively more explicit as the shopper approaches purchase.

Project modePortfolio
flagship

Static HTML/CSS/JS prototype with real browser-local commerce behavior.

Primary usersFashion-led
shoppers

People who value editorial discovery but still need size, fit, material and service clarity.

Core journeyDiscover →
decide → buy

Editorial entry, product discovery, size/fit decision, Bag, checkout and confirmation.

PlatformResponsive
web

1440 / 1280 / 768 / 390 / 320 representative contracts.

Problem

Luxury expression can create desire while still leaving purchase questions unresolved.

Size, fit, material, availability, delivery and returns become more important as commitment increases.

Goal

Preserve an authored fashion-house point of view without hiding commerce affordances or system reality.

Home and Collections carry stronger editorial expression; PDP, Bag and Checkout become more explicit and task-focused.

Role

Product/UX/UI direction, art direction, interaction system, responsive behavior, coded prototype and cloud QA evidence.

Scope

Home, Collections, Shop, Product Detail, Saved, Bag, Checkout, Confirmation, Orders, Account, Client Services and policy/support routes.

System truth

Search/filter, Saved, Bag and local order records work in-browser. Payment, fulfilment, email and live inventory/authentication are not connected.

Evidence boundary

No participant research, conversion lift or production-commerce outcome is claimed. Current research is benchmark and expert-design evidence.
