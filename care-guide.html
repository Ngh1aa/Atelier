GARMENT CARE

Care is a continuation
of design.

A small number of considered habits can preserve the texture, proportion and character of a favourite piece for years to come.

ATELIER/Care Guide

THE ATELIER PRINCIPLE

Wear with intention. Care with patience.

Refer first to the sewn-in care label on each garment. The guidelines below are general recommendations and never replace the instructions supplied with a specific piece.

REST

Allow fibres to recover

Air knitwear, tailoring and leather accessories between wears. Avoid over-cleaning pieces that benefit from rest.

STORE

Protect shape and texture

Fold knitwear, hang tailoring on broad hangers and keep pieces away from direct sunlight, humidity and heat.

CLEAN

Choose specialist care

For delicate, structured or natural-fibre pieces, use a reputable professional cleaner familiar with the garment’s construction.

BY MATERIAL

A concise care reference

The product label and product description remain the primary source for a specific piece.

WOOL & CASHMERE

Brush, air, fold

Remove surface dust with a soft garment brush and rest pieces between wears. Fold to avoid shoulder stretch.

SILK & VISCOSE

Handle with a light touch

Avoid friction, strong fragrance and prolonged light exposure. Consult a specialist before cleaning.

LEATHER & SUEDE

Keep dry and supported

Protect from water and store with internal support. Use a leather specialist for cleaning or conditioning.
