How do I choose a size?

Start with the Size Guide, then check the fit note on the product page. Product pages remain the source of truth for the piece you are viewing.

Can I return a piece?

The current ATELIER prototype presents a 14-day return or exchange policy. Review the Shipping & Returns page before checkout for the full conditions shown by this site.

How should I care for it?

Follow the garment care label first. The Care Guide explains the general handling principles used across the catalogue.

Where are my saved pieces and orders?

Saved pieces and locally recorded order information are available from this device through Saved and Account.
