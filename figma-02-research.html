Insight 01

Editorial ≠ ambiguity

Luxury can stay visually restrained while price, size, state and service remain explicit.

Insight 02

Fit is a decision surface

Size guide, model notes and fit information belong beside size selection, not in detached help content.

Insight 03

Unavailable should stay visible

Sold-out or unknown states should be understandable rather than silently disappearing.

Insight 04

Commerce density can rise

As the shopper moves toward checkout, practical facts should outweigh campaign theatre.

Opportunity statement: build a fashion-commerce system where expressive imagery creates desire at discovery, then size, fit, variant, availability and service evidence progressively reduce uncertainty before commitment.
